Heavy equipment rental quietly underpins a wider range of Indian industry than most people outside construction and infrastructure realize. Here's a look at the sectors CE Infrastructure LLP actively serves, and the equipment categories each one typically relies on.

## Construction

Construction is the most visible and highest-volume user of rental equipment, covering everything from facade access and structural steel erection to material handling and concrete placement. Boom lifts and scissor lifts handle safe access solutions for high-rise building work and interior finishing, while cranes manage structural lifts and material positioning across a site. See our [boom lift rental guide](/blog/boom-lift-rental-india-types-costs-how-to-choose) for the access equipment side of this work.

## Power

Power sector projects, spanning turbine maintenance, solar panel installation, wind turbine service, and cooling tower work, depend heavily on aerial work platforms to reach equipment at height safely, often in operational plant environments where downtime and safety margins matter enormously. This is one of the sectors where certified equipment and certified operators, covered in our [IPAF certification guide](/blog/ipaf-certification-explained), carry particular weight given the operational risk involved.

## Telecom

Telecom infrastructure work, including tower installation, network equipment setup, and antenna deployment, often relies on truck-mounted boom lifts for their combination of extended reach and road mobility between sites spread across a region. Our [truck-mounted boom lift guide](/blog/truck-mounted-boom-lifts-explained) covers why this equipment category suits telecom work specifically.

## Railways and Metro

Rail and metro infrastructure projects, particularly pier erection, girder launching, and station construction, are among the most structurally demanding applications in this list, requiring crawler crane capacity and specialized lifting equipment matched to precise engineering tolerances. Our [pier girder erection guide](/blog/pier-girder-erection-process) covers this process in full.

## Oil and Gas

Refinery and petrochemical plant work, including plant maintenance, pipeline inspection, tank cleaning, and emergency repairs, places a premium on reliable, well-maintained equipment and rapid response capability, since downtime in an active industrial facility carries significant operational cost. This sector's safety and compliance expectations closely mirror what we cover in our [CE certification guide](/blog/ce-certification-explained).

## Manufacturing

Manufacturing and industrial facility work, covering equipment installation, warehouse operations, and facility maintenance, frequently calls on pick & carry cranes for their combination of lifting capability and maneuverability inside active production environments. See our [pick & carry vs. crawler crane comparison](/blog/pick-and-carry-crane-vs-crawler-crane) for how this equipment category fits active industrial sites specifically.

## Ports and Shipyards

Port and shipyard work, including ship repairs, drydock operations, and port equipment support, is one of CE Infrastructure LLP's most differentiated service areas, combining marine access equipment like work barges and spider lifts with the broader crane and lifting capability needed for port infrastructure. Our [drydock vs. afloat ship repair guide](/blog/drydock-vs-afloat-ship-repair) and [work barges and spider lifts guide](/blog/work-barges-and-spider-lifts) cover this work in detail.

## FAQs

**What industries use crane rental the most in India?**
Construction, railways and metro infrastructure, manufacturing, and oil and gas are among the heaviest users, though demand spans nearly every heavy-industry sector.

**Does one rental company typically serve all these industries?**
Capability varies significantly by provider. Serving this full range well requires genuine breadth across equipment categories, certified crews, and pan-India logistics, not every rental company maintains all three.

**Which industry has the most specialized equipment needs?**
Ports and shipyards, and rail/metro infrastructure, tend to require the most specialized equipment, given marine access challenges and precision structural lifting requirements respectively.

**How do I know what equipment my industry actually needs?**
The honest answer is that it depends on the specific task, not just the industry label, describing the actual work to a rental partner with genuine cross-industry experience is more reliable than assuming based on sector alone.
